NWN Network — Privacy Policy

Who we are. NWN Network (YouTube channel @NeoWNN) publishes original AI-generated animated news videos. This policy covers the internal upload tool ("NWN publisher") that uses the YouTube API Services to publish our own videos to our own channel.

What the tool does. It uploads videos we made to the NWN Network channel, sets their title, description, tags, language, category and privacy status, and updates our channel description. It has one user: the channel owner, who authorized it once with Google OAuth.

What data we access. Only our own channel's basic information and the videos we upload. We do not access, collect, store or share any other YouTube user's data, comments, subscribers or analytics through the API.

What we store. One OAuth refresh token, kept in a private file (owner-only permissions) on our own server, used solely to upload and manage our own videos. We keep a local log of our uploaded video IDs.

Sharing. We do not sell or share any data obtained through the YouTube API Services with third parties.

Deletion. The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions ; we delete the stored token on revocation or on request.

Google and YouTube. This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
